Gradr — Your AI Career Command Center

Gradr is your AI career command center for resume analysis, job matching, applications, and interview coaching. It is a career platform for job seekers, students, graduates and career changers — not a grading, marking or test-score tool.

What Gradr does

  • Resume analysis and ATS optimization — upload a resume and get an ATS compatibility score, keyword gaps, and AI rewrite suggestions.
  • AI job matching — score your profile against live job openings and see exactly how to close the gap for each role.
  • Job applications — generate tailored cover letters and recruiter outreach, then track every application through an offer pipeline.
  • AI mock interviews and interview coaching — practise realtime voice interviews and get a scored debrief with concrete coaching feedback.
  • Career development — skill gap analysis and a personalised roadmap to your next role.
